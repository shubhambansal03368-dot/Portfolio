04 // Engineering Work

Projects & Real-World Problem Solving

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 Milestone

LEGALSCAN

Role: Team Leader (SIH 2026)

A technology-driven solution designed to analyze packaged product labels and identify potential compliance discrepancies under India's Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules.

01. Problem Statement & Scope
Packaged commodity regulations mandate strict statutory label declarations: Maximum Retail Price (MRP), net quantity, manufacturer/packer details, dates of manufacture/import, and consumer helpline data. Manual audits are slow, error-prone, and impossible to scale across millions of commercial SKUs.
02. Implemented Features (Prototype)
  • Image ingestion pipeline for packaging label captures.
  • Text region detection and Optical Character Recognition (OCR) conceptual flow.
  • Algorithmic rule validator parsing extracted text against statutory checklists.
  • Automated discrepancy flagging for missing or non-compliant mandatory declarations.
03. My Role as Team Leader
  • Led the project from conception: deconstructed complex Ministry regulatory requirements into actionable engineering milestones.
  • Architected the workflow pipeline connecting label capture, text extraction, and rule validation.
  • Coordinated sprint tasks, maintained team momentum under hackathon deadlines, and led the technical evaluation presentation.
04. Future Roadmap (Clearly Separated)
  • On-Device Inference: Developing lightweight edge OCR models for instantaneous offline camera scanning.
  • Multi-Lingual Script Parsing: Expanding extraction support for regional Indian scripts across diverse states.
  • Registry Verification: Connecting with centralized government inspection databases and barcode standards.

Leading with Ownership & Clarity

True leadership isn't a title; it's the willingness to take full accountability, eliminate ambiguity, and bring out the best in a technical team.

During the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026, I had the privilege of stepping up as Team Leader for our project, LEGALSCAN. Operating in an intense hackathon environment with strict time limits tested not only our coding abilities, but our capacity to communicate clearly and think on our feet.

As leader, my primary goal was to prevent confusion. I broke down an ambiguous government problem statement from the Ministry of Consumer Affairs regarding legal metrology into modular, concrete technical tasks that each team member could execute with clarity.

When our text recognition and compliance rules clashed on edge cases, I facilitated focused discussions to resolve design trade-offs without losing momentum. This experience cemented my understanding that great engineering requires empathy, active listening, and collective accountability.
